The idea of the game

In short: take land, earn money with it, build an army, win the war.

Who plays?

Each player leads one or more nations. The nations take turns, always in the same order. When every nation has had its turn, one round is over.

How do I get money?

The map is cut into territories. Each territory shows a number: that is how much money it brings you every turn. The money is called PUs (production units).

 +   = 5 PUs  →  

Two territories worth 3 and 2 bring 5 PUs: enough for one infantry at your factory.

With your PUs you buy new units: soldiers, tanks, ships and planes. The more territories you hold, the bigger your army can grow.

How do I win?

Every map says what you need, usually a number of victory cities or the capitals of the enemy. Hold them, and you win.
