Your first game

TripleA is a turn based strategy game: every nation moves in turn, and each turn has the same phases. You win by taking enough important territories, usually the capitals and victory cities of the other side.

Start a game against the computer

  1. In the main menu, choose Play locally.
  2. On the left you see the selected game. Change game picks another map; for a first game the small Tutorial map is the best choice. If it is missing, get it in Maps.
  3. Above the list of nations, click a quick setup button such as Play Allies: you play every nation of that side, the computer plays the others.
  4. Click Start Game.

Find your way on the screen

The map fills most of the window. Drag it with the mouse or use the arrow keys, zoom with the mouse wheel. On the right, the turn guide says whose turn it is, which phase runs and what to do in it. Below it, the tabs show the actions of the phase, the players, resources and the territory under the mouse.

Take your time: the computer waits for you in every phase. When you are done with a phase, click Done.
