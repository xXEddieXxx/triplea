A turn, step by step

Most maps use the same order of phases. The turn guide always shows where you are.

  1. Research (if the game has technology): spend money on dice for new technology.
  2. Purchase: buy new units with your income, shown in PUs (production units). They appear at the end of the turn.
  3. Combat move: move units into enemy territories or sea zones to attack.
  4. Combat: every attack is fought out. Click a battle to start it, then roll the dice.
  5. Non-combat move: move the units that did not attack to safe places, eg to defend your front or to land your planes.
  6. Place: put the units you bought into territories with a factory.
  7. End turn: you collect your income from the territories you hold.

Tips

  • A unit cannot move in the combat move and again in the non-combat move.
  • Planes must be able to reach a friendly territory or carrier after combat.
  • Made a mistake? In the move phases, Undo takes back a move.
