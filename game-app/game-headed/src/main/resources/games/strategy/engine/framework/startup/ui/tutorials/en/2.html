A turn, phase by phase

In short: buy, attack, fight, move the rest, place what you bought.

Your turn has the same steps every time. They are called phases. The bar at the top of the game window always shows which phase you are in. When you are done with a phase, click Done.

  1. Buy (purchase): choose new units and pay with your PUs. They wait until step 5.
  2. Attack (combat move): move units onto enemy land or sea to attack it.
  3. Fight (battles): each attack is fought out with dice.
  4. Move (non-combat move): move units that did not attack, and fly your planes home.
  5. Place: put the units you bought onto territories with a factory.
  6. End turn: you get the PUs of all your territories. Then the next nation plays.

Some maps have extra phases, eg research. You only see them when the map uses them.
