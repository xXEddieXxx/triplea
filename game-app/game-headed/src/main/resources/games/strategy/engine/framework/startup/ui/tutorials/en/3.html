Combat

A battle happens when units move into a territory or sea zone held by the enemy. It is fought in rounds until one side is gone or the attacker retreats.

How a round works

  1. Every unit rolls one die. A unit hits when the die shows its attack value or less (when attacking) or its defense value or less (when defending). An infantry attacking with 1 hits only on a 1, a tank attacking with 3 hits on 1, 2 or 3.
  2. Each hit takes out one unit of the other side. The defender chooses which of its units are lost, and so do you when you are hit.
  3. After each round the attacker may retreat to a territory it came from, or fight on.

Good to know

  • Some units fire first, like submarines, or only at planes, like anti aircraft guns.
  • Cheap units such as infantry are usually lost first, to keep the strong ones.
  • Before you attack, the battle calculator in the Game menu estimates your chances.
