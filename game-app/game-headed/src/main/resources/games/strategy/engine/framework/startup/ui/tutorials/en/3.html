How combat works

In short: every unit rolls a die. A low roll is a hit.

Who hits?

Every unit has two numbers: one for attack and one for defense. In a fight, each unit rolls one die. If the die shows its number or less, it hits.

1234 56Infantry attacks with 1: only a 1 hits
1 2 3456 A tank attacks with 3: 1, 2 or 3 hits
1 2 3 456 A fighter defends with 4: 1 to 4 hits

What happens then?

  • Every hit kills one unit of the other side. Each player picks which of his units die; most pick the cheapest first.
  • Then the next round starts. After each round the attacker may retreat.
  • The fight ends when one side has no units left. If your land units win, the territory is yours.
