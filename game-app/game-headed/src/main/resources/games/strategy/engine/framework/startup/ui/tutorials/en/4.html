Sea, air and transports

In short: ships carry your army over water; planes fly far but must land again.

Crossing the sea

carries or

Soldiers and tanks cannot swim. A transport ship carries them:

  1. Move the transport into the sea next to your units.
  2. Move the units onto it.
  3. Sail to the coast you want and unload them there.

Transports cannot fight. Keep warships near them.

Planes

carries

Fighters and bombers fly far and attack land and sea. But at the end of your turn they must land on your own land or on a carrier. A plane that cannot land is lost.

moves 4: 2 out to fight, 2 back home

Good to know

  • You attack the sea like land: move your ships into it.
  • Submarines shoot first and can dive away. A destroyer stops that.
