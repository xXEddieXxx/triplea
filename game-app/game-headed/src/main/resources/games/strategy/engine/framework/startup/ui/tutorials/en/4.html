Units

Every map has its own units, but most follow the same idea: land units hold territories, ships control the sea, planes strike far.

  • Infantry: cheap, defends well, the backbone of every army.
  • Artillery and tanks: stronger in attack; tanks move two territories.
  • Fighters and bombers: fly far and attack land and sea, but must land after combat.
  • Transports: carry land units over the sea; they barely defend themselves.
  • Battleships, cruisers, destroyers, submarines and carriers: fight at sea, some can bombard the coast before a landing.
  • Factories: you can only place new units in territories with one.
