Playing online

TripleA is at its best against other people. In the main menu, choose Multiplayer.

In the lobby

  1. Choose Play in the lobby and sign in. Without an account you play as a guest; an account keeps your name.
  2. The list shows the games that are hosted now. Games waiting for players come first. Select one and click Join game, or double click it.
  3. Or click Host game to start your own game for others to join.
  4. Say hello in the chat on the right. The players there are happy to help new players.

Other ways

  • Host or join in the local network: play with friends in the same network, or by address.
  • Play by email or by forum: play slowly, one turn at a time, whenever you have time.
