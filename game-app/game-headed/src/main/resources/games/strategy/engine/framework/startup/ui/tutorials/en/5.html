Your first game

In short: start a small game against the computer and try it out. You cannot break anything.

Start the game

  1. In the main menu, click Play locally.
  2. On the left, click Change map and pick the small Tutorial map. Not there? Get it in Maps.
  3. Click Play Allies (or another side): you play that side, the computer plays the other.
  4. Click Start Game.

Move your units

Click a unit stack: each click takes one more unit. The units you took follow your mouse.

Then click where they should go. The red arrow shows the way.

  • Ctrl-click takes the whole stack. Right-click puts one unit back.
  • Made a mistake? Undo takes the move back.
  • Finished? Done ends the phase.

Look around

  • Hold the right mouse button to drag the map. Ctrl and the mouse wheel zoom.
  • The bar at the top says whose turn it is and what to do now.
  • Take your time: the computer waits for you.
