Maps

Hundreds of maps made by players are free to download: world wars, ancient empires, fantasy and more. Each map can hold several games, eg different start years.

  1. In the main menu, choose Maps.
  2. New maps shows what you can get. Type in the search field to find a map by name or category; click a map to see its picture and description.
  3. Click Install. The download continues in the background while you look around.
  4. To play it, choose Play locally and then Change game.

Under Installed you can remove maps. When a map you have gets a new version, it shows up under Updates available.

Want to make your own map? Make your own map at the bottom of the Maps page starts the map creator.
