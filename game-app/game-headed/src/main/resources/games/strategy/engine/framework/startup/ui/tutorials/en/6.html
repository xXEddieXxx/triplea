Playing with others

In short: click Multiplayer in the main menu and pick how you want to play.

  vs  

Play now, together

  • Play in the lobby: meet players from all over the world. Sign in, or play as a guest. Join a game that waits for players, or host your own. Questions? Ask in the chat.
  • Host a game / Join a game: play with friends. One hosts, the others join with the host's address.

Play one turn at a time

  • Play by forum: play your turn whenever you have time. TripleA posts it to the forum, then the next player takes his turn. You need an account on forums.triplea-game.org.
  • Play by email: the same, but your turn goes by email.

In forum and email games, a dice server rolls the dice for everyone, so nobody can cheat. On the setup screen, click Register once for each player's email.
