Tips for your first games

  • Infantry is cheap and defends well. Buy mostly infantry, plus some artillery and tanks to attack.
  • Attack with more. Two against one is safe. One against one is luck.
  • Guard your capital. If you lose it, you lose your money.
  • Think ahead. New units only appear at factories. Buy where you will need them.
  • Try things. Until you click Done, Undo takes your moves back. Only a battle, once fought, counts.

More maps

There are hundreds of free maps. Click Maps in the main menu, find one you like and click Install. To play it, click Change map before you start a game.
