Play by forum

In a game by forum, everyone plays his turn on his own computer, whenever he has time. Your turn goes to a thread of the forum forums.triplea-game.org, in the category Play By Forum: a summary of what happened and the save game. The next player opens that save, plays his turn and posts it. A dice server rolls the dice and mails the rolls to all players, so nobody can cheat.

Before your first game

  1. Register on forums.triplea-game.org to get a forum account.
  2. In the main menu, choose Multiplayer and then Play by forum. Click Sign in with the forum: the forum's own login page opens in a window of TripleA. Sign in there as usual. TripleA keeps the sign-in; it does not store your password.
  3. The dice server needs the email addresses of all players. Each address is registered once with the MARTI dice server: use the Register button on the setup screen of a new game.

Start a game

  1. On the Play by forum page, click + New game.
  2. Choose the map and which nations each player plays.
  3. Choose the thread for the game: a new one is created for you, or pick one that exists.
  4. Enter the email addresses for the dice.
  5. Click Start Game and play your first turn. At its end, TripleA posts the turn summary and the save game to the thread.

Take your turn

My games lists your games; those waiting for you are marked Your turn. Open the game, click Open save game on the newest post, play and end your turn: TripleA posts it.

Watch, join and talk

Browse games shows the other games of the category. View opens a save to look at only; nothing is played or posted. Watch follows a game: the forum tells you of new posts, and the game shows under My games. Join game writes a request to join into the answer box: add which nation you can take over and send it. To answer in a thread, write in the box below its posts; the bar above it formats your text and attaches pictures, as the forum's editor does.

Tips

  • The first posts of a new forum member wait until a moderator approves them.
  • If you do not have the map of a game, TripleA downloads it by itself.
  • If something fails, Open in browser shows the game's thread in the forum, which always works.
